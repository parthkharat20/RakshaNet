RAKSHANET IN-DEPTH SYSTEM STORYBOOK CHAPTER 1: THE REAL CRIME & THE GOLDEN WINDOW

1. The Anatomy of Modern Cyber Scams in India

1.1 Real-World Scams Affecting Millions

Every day in India, over 50,000 citizens fall victim to digital scams. The money stolen is not from multinational corporations—it is the life savings of pensioners, tuition fees of college students, and emergency funds of middle-class families. Criminal syndicates primarily operate four psychological traps:

1. The Electricity / Utility Bill Threat: Victims receive an urgent SMS: "Electricity will be disconnected at 9 PM tonight due to unpaid bill." In panic, they call a fake helpline, download an app, or scan a small UPI QR code for a "₹10 verification fee". The app steals their credentials or records their UPI PIN, immediately looting their savings account.
2. The "Digital Arrest" Extortion Scheme: Scammers pose as CBI, Mumbai Police, or Customs officers over WhatsApp/Skype video calls wearing realistic police uniforms. They falsely claim a FedEx parcel with illegal narcotics was seized in the victim's name and intimidate the victim into transferring their entire bank balance to a "secret government verification account".
3. The Telegram Part-Time Job / Task Trap: Young job-seekers are offered ₹3,000/day to "like YouTube videos" or "review hotels on Google Maps". After small initial payouts to build trust, they are lured into depositing lakhs into fake cryptocurrency trading portals that block withdrawals.

1.2 The "Golden Window" (180 to 240 Minutes)

When a victim loses money, what happens during the next 3 hours? Cybercriminals operate a synchronized 3-stage relay race:

Timeline Scammer Actions Traditional Police/Bank Status
0 – 15 Minutes Stolen money hits the 1st "Mule" bank account. Victim is in shock, attempting to call bank phone helplines.
15 – 45 Minutes Money is divided into 4 smaller transfers across 3 different banks (Layering). Victim dials 1930 or visits the local police station to file an offline complaint.
45 – 180 Minutes Funds pool into a final account. A cash runner on a motorbike receives an alert. Police officer begins writing down the incident by hand. No communication has reached the beneficiary bank.
180 – 240 Minutes Runner inserts cloned ATM cards and withdraws untraceable paper currency notes. The money has physically exited the banking wall. Digital trail is dead.
Day 2 to Day 4 Scammers divide cash in safe houses. Ghost accounts discarded. Formal police freeze email arrives at the bank. The account is frozen with ₹0 balance.

1.3 Why Traditional Banking Rules Are Completely Blind to This

The Fatal Flaw of Existing Bank Anti-Fraud Software:
Standard banking security software (used by major banks worldwide) relies on historical behavior. If an account has transacted ₹10,000 every month for 5 years and suddenly sends ₹10,00,000, it flags an alert. However, criminal syndicates never use aged personal accounts. They recruit poor laborers or students in rural districts, pay them ₹2,000 to open fresh accounts with clean identity documents, and rent the debit cards. Because these accounts have zero history, the bank's computers see nothing abnormal. To existing software, the account looks completely clean!
